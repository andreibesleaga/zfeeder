# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason and, where you can, a suggested alternative.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project's scope.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience, different first languages
  and different amounts of time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing
others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other project space,
and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/andreibesleaga). Reports are read by the maintainer only.
The response will be proportionate: usually a private word, and in serious or repeated
cases a block from the repository.

This document is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
shortened to match a project of this size.
